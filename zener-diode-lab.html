Foundation

Why a Zener diode when the PN-junction diode already exists?

A Zener diode is not a different basic junction. It is a specially designed PN junction intended to operate safely in reverse breakdown.

Ordinary PN-junction diode

Main use: forward conduction for rectification and switching.
  • Forward bias reduces the junction barrier.
  • Current rises strongly after the forward knee.
  • Reverse current is very small before breakdown.
  • Its reverse breakdown is normally not the intended operating region.

Zener diode

Main special use: controlled reverse breakdown for voltage reference / regulation.
  • It is a heavily doped PN junction.
  • The depletion region is comparatively thin.
  • Reverse electric field becomes very strong near the designed breakdown voltage.
  • In the useful breakdown region, current changes markedly while voltage remains approximately VZ.

Carrier physics

Drift and diffusion are common to both

The basic transport physics is the same because both devices contain a PN junction.

Diffusion

Cause: carrier concentration gradient.

Electrons diffuse from the N side toward the P side; holes diffuse from the P side toward the N side. This initial carrier movement helps form the depletion region.

The repeatable method

Step-by-step regulator procedure

  1. Temporarily remove the Zener branch and calculate Vopen=ViRL/(RS+RL).
  2. If Vopen<VZ, the Zener is OFF and Vout=Vopen.
  3. If Vopen≥VZ, test the breakdown solution Vout≈VZ.
  4. Calculate IR=(Vi−VZ)/RS.
  5. Calculate IL=VZ/RL.
  6. Then IZ=IR−IL. Verify IZ and Zener power.

Find RL,min and RL,max

Use Vi=50 V, RS=1 kΩ, VZ=10 V and IZM=32 mA.

IR=(50−10)/1 kΩ = 40 mA

Worked example

Do the ON/OFF prediction first — then solve

This follows the 16 V, 1 kΩ, 3 kΩ, 10 V example from the notes.

Given
Vi=16 V
RS=1 kΩ
RL=3 kΩ
VZ=10 V
First ignore the Zener: Vopen=16×3/(1+3)=12 V. Since 12 V>10 V, the Zener can turn ON.
  1. Vout=VZ=10 V.
  2. VR=Vi−VZ=16−10=6 V.
  3. IR=6/1 kΩ=6 mA.
  4. IL=10/3 kΩ=3.33 mA.
  5. IZ=6−3.33=2.67 mA.
  6. PZ=10×2.67 mA=26.7 mW.
Final: VL=10 V, IR=6 mA, IL=3.33 mA, IZ=2.67 mA, PZ=26.7 mW.
